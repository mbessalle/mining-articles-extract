# Superior Mining Receives Final Approval to Proceed with Vieux Comptoir Property Acquisition

VANCOUVER, BC, April 14, 2023 /CNW/ - **Superior Mining International Corp.**("**Superior Mining** " or the **"Company"**) (TSXV: SUI) (OTC: SUIFF) announces that, further to its news release of January 26, 2023, it has obtained final approval from the TSX Venture Exchange (the "**TSXV** ") to proceed with the option agreement (the "**Option Agreement** ") with Quebec Pegmatite Corp. (the "Vendor"), a subsidiary of Coloured Ties Capital Inc. ("**CTC** "), whereby Superior Mining has the option to earn a 100% interest in the Vieux Comptoir Lithium Property in Quebec (the "**Property** ") subject to meeting certain conditions. The 7,500,000 common shares due on TSXV approval have been issued to the Vendor. In addition, the Company paid aggregate finder's fees of 300,000 common shares to to two parties in respect of the Option Agreement. The common shares issued to the Vendor and the finder's shares are all subject to a hold period expiring four months from the date of closing.

[ ](https://mma.prnewswire.com/media/2054085/Superior_Mining_International_Corporation_Superior_Mining_Receiv.html)

The Company has provided the TSXV with a National Instrument 43-101 technical report in respect of the Property as requested. For additional information on the Property and the Option Agreement, please refer to the Company's December 7, 2022 news release, which is available on SEDAR.
