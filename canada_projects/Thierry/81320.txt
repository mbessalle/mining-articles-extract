# Orecap Completes Sale of Cuprum to XXIX Metal Corp.

**Highlights:‎**

  * **Orecap's Portfolio company Cuprum acquired by XXIX Metal Corp. ‎in an all-share deal.**
  * **Orecap's now owns 15.1% if XXIX**
  * **XXIX becomes one of Eastern Canada's largest copper developer.**



Toronto, Ontario--(Newsfile Corp. - December 9, 2024) - Orecap Invest Corp. (TSXV: OCI) (OTCQB: ORFDF) (the "Company" or "Orecap") is pleased to announce that its portfolio company, XXIX Metal Corp. (formerly QC Copper and Gold Inc‎.) ‎("XXIX"), has acquired ‎100% of the issued and outstanding common shares ("Cuprum Shares") of Cuprum Corp. ("Cuprum")‎, establishing XXIX as one of Canada's largest copper resource developers, with two multi-billion-pound ‎copper deposits in Quebec and Ontario (the "Transaction").

Pursuant to the Transaction, XXIX acquired all the issued and outstanding Cuprum Shares, including the ‎29,500,000‎ Cuprum Shares (37.5%) owned by Orecap, in consideration for the issuance of 1.1538 common shares of XXIX ("XXIX Shares") for each one ‎Cuprum Share, based on the XXIX Share price of $0.13. Prior to completion of the Transaction‎, Orecap owned 5,059,752 XXIX Shares (representing 2.9% of the outstanding XXIX Shares) and following the completion of the Transaction, Orecap owns ‎34,037,100 XXIX Shares (representing 15.1% of the outstanding XXIX Shares). 

Orecap: (i) acquired the XXIX Shares in ‎connection with the Transaction; (ii) holds the XXIX Shares for investment purposes; ‎and (iii) does not have any current intentions to increase or decrease its beneficial ownership or ‎control or direction over any additional securities of XXIX. Orecap may, from time to time and depending on market and other conditions, acquire ‎additional XXIX Shares through market transactions, private agreements, treasury issuances, ‎dividend reinvestment programs, exercise of options, convertible securities or otherwise, or may sell ‎all or some portion of the XXIX Shares its owns or controls, or may continue to hold the ‎ XXIX Shares.‎ An early warning report will be filed by ‎Orecap in accordance with applicable securities laws and will be ‎available on SEDAR+ at [www.sedarplus.ca](https://api.newsfilecorp.com/redirect/arNBouwqRN).‎
