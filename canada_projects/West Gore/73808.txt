# Molten Metals Announces Completion of Asset Purchase Agreement with Military Metals Corp.

[](http://www.moltenmetalscorp.com/)  
---  
[](https://thenewswire.com/api/apps/tnw/r?i=1kEjFEOVv&m=twi)| [](https://thenewswire.com/api/apps/tnw/r?i=1kEjFEOVv&m=lin)|   
    

Vancouver, British Columbia / October 4, 2024 – TheNewswire - Molten Metals Corp. (the “Company”) (CSE:MOLT; FSE:Y44), announces that it has completed the sale of its West Gore Antimony Project, located in Nova Scotia, to Military Metals Corp. in consideration for a cash payment of $85,000, as originally announced on October 1, 2024.
