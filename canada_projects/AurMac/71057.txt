# Banyan Completes 75% Earn-In on the Aurex Property, Yukon

**VANCOUVER, BC / ACCESSWIRE / September 11, 2024 / Banyan Gold Corp** . (the "**Company** " or "**Banyan** ") (**TSXV:BYN**) is pleased to announce that it has completed the 75% earn in on the Aurex Property, comprised of 433 claims, which contains the majority of the Powerline Deposit at the AurMac Project (total 907 claims). Banyan has completed all the exploration expenditure requirements to earn 100%.

The Company completed the 75% ownership of the McQuesten Property which contains the Airstrip Deposit (73 claims) in December 2023, and has until December 2025 to complete a PEA and finalize the 100% ownership. The remaining 401 claims of the AurMac Project are 100% owned by Banyan.

_"Formalizing our 75% ownership of the Aurex Property is an important milestone,"_ stated Tara Christie, President and CEO, _"On Feb 6, 2024 the AurMac Resource was increased to 7.0 million gold ounces (see Table 1 below) which followed up on positive Metallurgical Test results announced February 6, 2024. The Company has a healthy treasury which is helping fund the ongoing 20,000 metres of targeted drilling and has ongoing technical work and metallurgy to support the PEA being completed by December 2025."_

** _About Banyan_**  
Banyan's primary asset, the **AurMac Project** is located in Canada's Yukon Territory. The current inferred Mineral Resource Estimate **("MRE** ") for the AurMac Project of 7.0 million ounces has an effective date of February 6, 2024.

The 173 square kilometres ("**sq km** ") AurMac Project lies 40 km from Mayo, Yukon. The AurMac Project is transected by the main Yukon highway and benefits from a 3-phase powerline, existing power station and cell phone coverage. Banyan has the right to earn up to a 100% interest, in both the Aurex and McQuesten Properties respectively, subject to certain royalties.

The inferred MRE for the AurMac Project was prepared on February 6, 2024, and consisted of **7,003,000** ounces of gold (see Table 1) hosted within near surface, road accessible pit constrained Mineral Resources contained in two near/on-surface deposits: the Airstrip, and Powerline Deposits.
