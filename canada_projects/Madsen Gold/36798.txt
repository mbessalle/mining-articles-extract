# West Red Lake Gold Mines Ltd. Acquisition of Madsen Gold Project Approved by Court

**NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION TO U.S. NEWS WIRE SERVICES OR DISSEMINATION IN THE UNITED STATES**

VANCOUVER, British Columbia, May 30, 2023 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- **West Red Lake Gold Mines Ltd.** (“**West Red Lake Gold** ” or “**WRLG** ” or the “Company”) **(TSXV: WRLG**) **(OTCQB: WRLGF)** announces that the British Columbia Supreme Court has approved the acquisition by the Company of all of the Madsen Gold Project through the acquisition of all of the outstanding shares of Pure Gold Mining Inc. (“Pure Gold”) (the “Acquisition”) pursuant to an Approval and Reverse Vesting Order (the “Order”) in Pure Gold’s proceedings under the _Companies’ Creditors Arrangement Act_. The Order approved the transactions contemplated in the Share Purchase Agreement entered into between the Company, Pure Gold and a fund managed by Sprott Resource Lending Corp. dated May 17, 2023.  Details of the Acquisition and the Share Purchase Agreement can be found in the Company’s [April 17, 2023](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=qjqm33tKw74b44KR3_EvbA787tFC4TZKYJiQKewZGJp3ui4gqbzPE4JmyBuAZmtukVvLymU4t1NaPmV9_uf9d9bbRaJIlTXrTWsM2XZ68-Zi7jLRqZIWQE2ry_DWxpB5QlkQ75jiLuM8Ydq0E2X_FhXg0mpCi4ZQ748pnLQfnxjgMFKxNBPMCRE0JXqLpXriK08xZXd6xTcdsqeqXCzXKw== "April 17, 2023") and [May 18, 2023](https://www.globenewswire.com/Tracker?data=2whJDSwjtpESWJa_J-UERWs2EpUfthREsd69Ug1vblliyBaqUzurdqzTpF9-HHQxZzR_zb7wN62QbIHv_oVy0t_XVxWPb-5b8XlDkhLeFUgUwHe3RtVS-tnftuyHjydhERL-jI0aFvKqU84c_yZEHZ90T3fgJ1h3GyHU6ESsAJMmWRenFjJlwfvuanmOSf-k "May 18, 2023") news releases, which are available on the Company’s website and on SEDAR.

Closing of the Acquisition is subject to various conditions precedent including receipt of all required regulatory approvals including the approval of the TSX Venture Exchange. The Company is working towards closing the Acquisition prior to June 28, 2023.

**ABOUT WEST RED LAKE GOLD MINES LTD.**

West Red Lake Gold Mines Ltd. is a mineral exploration company that is publicly traded and dedicated to creating value for its shareholders by discovering new gold mines in the highly productive Red Lake Gold District of Northwest Ontario, Canada. This district has yielded 30 million ounces of gold from high-grade zones and hosts some of the world's richest gold deposits. WRLG holds an expansive property position spanning 3,100 ha in Red Lake, the Rowan Project, including three former gold mines - Rowan, Mount Jamie, and Red Summit. The Rowan Project covers a 12-km strike length along the Pipestone Bay St. Paul Deformation Zone and WRLG plans to continue exploring this property along strike and to depth aggressively in 2023.
