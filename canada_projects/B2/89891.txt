# Steadright 50% Acquisition of B2 Mineral Claims and Private Placement

[](http://www.steadright.ca/)  
---  
   

March 3rd, 2025 – TheNewswire - Muskoka, Ontario – Steadright Critical Minerals Inc. (CSE: SCM) (“Steadright” or the “Company”) is pleased to announce that it has agreed to acquire 50% interest in 59 claims known as the B2 project in Saguenay-Lac-St-Jean Region of Québec, through a purchase agreement with Critical Foundation Metals Inc. for consideration of 1,600,000 common shares of Steadright Critical Minerals. 

 

Grab bag samples, taken independently, in October of 2022 on the B2 property yielded results with individual grades up to 3.19% Copper, 0.71% Nickel, 0.08% Cobalt and 7.25 g/t Silver

ABOUT STREADRIGHT CRITICAL MINERALS INC.

 

Steadright Critical Minerals Inc. is a mineral exploration company established in 2019. Steadright currently holds an option on its RAM property near Port Cartier, Quebec within the Côte-Nord Region, which is accessible by route 138. The RAM project is comprised of over 13,000 acres and located on an Anorthositic complex that is in a highly prospective geological unit and historically been under explored for Ni, Cu, Co and precious metals.
