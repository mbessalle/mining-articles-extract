# Canadian Copper Closes Chester Asset Sale and Receives Notice of Early Turgeon Payment

Toronto, Ontario--(Newsfile Corp. - September 30, 2024) - Canadian Copper Inc. (CSE: CCI) ("Canadian Copper" or the "Company") announced today the closing of the Chester Project asset sale and certain modifications to the [previously announced Raptor Resources Ltd. ("Raptor") transaction.](https://api.newsfilecorp.com/redirect/55QJyI4Knw) Raptor has requested (**Table A**) certain changes to the transaction agreements to which the Company has agreed to. The executed updated purchase agreements do not impact any cash component paid to Canadian Copper previously [announced on September 10th, 2024.](https://api.newsfilecorp.com/redirect/LG4RZsaX4M)

**Payment Transfer Summary**

  * The $675,0001 associated with the Chester Project Agreement is being transferred to the Company. 

  * The $225,0001 early [exercise payment associated](https://api.newsfilecorp.com/redirect/oLkOgSMk3O) with the Turgeon Project Agreement is being transferred to the Company. 

  * Under the modified agreements, Raptor is targeting its Initial Public Offering ("IPO") and listing on the Australian Securities Exchange ("ASX") in June 2025. Therefore, Canadian Copper will receive 4,000,000 Raptor unlisted shares until their listing is complete. Future Turgeon cash and share payment2 totals remain unchanged.

**About Canadian Copper Inc.**

Canadian Copper is a Canadian-based mineral exploration company with a copper and base metals portfolio of historical resources and grassroots projects. The Company is focused on the prolific Bathurst Mining Camp (BMC) of New Brunswick, Canada. There are currently 90,044,762 shares issued and outstanding in the Company.
